Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how OTP Email Service ("we," "our," or "the Service") handles information when you use our email delivery service. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

Information Collected

OTP Email Service collects and processes only the minimum information necessary to deliver one-time password emails:

  • Recipient Email Address: The email address to which the OTP is sent
  • OAuth Authentication Token: Used to authenticate with Google Gmail API

Important: We do not read, store, or access the content of any emails in your Gmail account. Our service only sends outgoing emails containing OTP codes.

How Information Is Used

The information collected is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Sending one-time password emails for authentication purposes
  • Authenticating with Google's Gmail API to enable email delivery
  • Maintaining service functionality and reliability

We do not use your information for marketing, profiling, or any purpose other than delivering OTP emails as requested.

Data Storage and Retention

OTP Email Service is designed with privacy in mind:

  • No personal data is permanently stored by our service
  • Email addresses are processed in real-time and not retained after delivery
  • OAuth tokens are stored securely and used only for Gmail API authentication
  • No email content or user data is logged or archived

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect information during processing:

  • Secure HTTPS connections for all communications
  • OAuth 2.0 authentication for Gmail API access
  • Minimal data collection principle - we only request the gmail.send scope
  • No storage of sensitive personal information

Third-Party Services

OTP Email Service uses the following third-party service:

Google Gmail API: We use Google's Gmail API to send emails. This requires OAuth 2.0 authentication with the gmail.send scope, which only allows sending emails on your behalf. We cannot read, modify, or delete any emails in your account.

Your use of Google services is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties except:

  • As required to use Google Gmail API for email delivery
  • If required by law or legal process

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Revoke OAuth access at any time through your Google Account settings
  • Request information about how your data is processed
  • Contact us with any privacy-related questions or concerns

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
